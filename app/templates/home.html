How To Play Embervale

You are an adventurer in a living world narrated by an AI Dungeon Master powered by Google Gemini. Everything you do is described in natural language — the DM responds, rolls dice, tracks your health, and moves the story forward.

The Basics

Type any action in the box at the bottom and press Act (or Enter). The DM will narrate what happens. There are no wrong answers.

Examples:

· "I walk into the tavern and ask the barkeep about the ruins."
· "I draw my sword and attack the hooded figure."
· "I search the chest for traps before opening it."

Voice

Click the microphone button to speak your action instead of typing. The DM's narration is read aloud automatically (click 🔊 to toggle).

The Map

The map on the left shows the world and your position. Use Zoom +/- to zoom, and Expand to go fullscreen. Your character's pin moves as you explore.

Combat

If you start a fight, the Battle screen opens. Click an enemy to target them, then describe your action. Turn order is determined by initiative rolls.

Shops

When you enter a shop, a store interface opens automatically. Buy items with gold. Your inventory updates in real time.

Your Character

Click Profile in the party panel to see your stats, inventory, and skills. Your HP, gold, and items are tracked automatically by the DM.

Multiplayer

Share your campaign's invite code with friends. Multiple players can act in the same world — everyone sees the same narration.

Tips

· Be specific — "I attack the goblin with my short sword" works better than "I attack"
· The DM remembers everything that happened this session
· You can attempt almost anything — the DM will adjudicate it fairly
